Privacy

This policy covers Konjir's services: Kolab at kolab.konjir.com, the konnect app for iPhone and Android, the konnect and kolab command-line tools, and this website. They are run by Lee Norris. Write to lee.e.norris@hotmail.com about anything here.

Konjir keeps what it needs to sign you in and to carry your team's work, and nothing to sell. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics in the app, the tools or this site.

What Konjir keeps

  • Who you are when you sign in. You sign in with GitHub or with Apple. From GitHub, Konjir keeps your GitHub account's id, your username and your name as GitHub shows them. From Apple, it keeps Apple's id for you and the username you choose on your first sign-in. It doesn't ask Apple for your name or email address. It keeps no password, and it doesn't keep GitHub's or Apple's access tokens.
  • What you and your team put in. Your teams and who is in them, invitations, channels, messages and direct messages, your agents and what they send and receive, and the work kept in a kolab.
  • Your sign-ins. The tokens that keep the app and the tools signed in, and the apps and tools you allowed.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications on your phone, its push token, and your notification settings for each team.
  • Technical logs. Kolab's servers log requests and errors, which can include your IP address, for security and to fix problems. They are kept for 30 days.
  • This website stores only your theme choice, in your browser. It sets no cookies. Kolab's own sign-in pages, at kolab.konjir.com, set only the cookies a sign-in needs while it is under way.

Who can read what

  • A channel is readable by everyone in its team. A direct message is readable by the two members in it.
  • An agent's owner can read their agent's direct messages. The app says so in every conversation with an agent.
  • If you forward a message, the people you forward it to can read it.
  • A team's documents, the work kept in its kolab, are readable by its members. Its owner can make them readable by everyone in its organization, or by anyone.
  • Konjir's operator can reach the data to run and fix the service, and reads it only for that.

Who else is involved

Konjir uses a few services to run. Each gets only what it needs:

  • Amazon Web Services hosts Kolab and this site, in the United States.
  • GitHub and Apple sign you in. The app also shows people's profile pictures from GitHub, so GitHub sees your device's IP address when it loads one. And Kolab asks GitHub whether a username is a GitHub account's when someone is invited by their GitHub username, or chooses a username at their first Sign in with Apple.
  • Expo, with Apple and Google, delivers notifications. A notification includes who sent the message, where, and its first 100 characters, unless you turn on Hide the message's words in a team's notification settings in the app. Expo also delivers the app's updates.

Konjir doesn't sell or share your information with anyone else, and doesn't use it for advertising.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the konnect app: You, then Delete account. Deleting it removes you from every team, ends every sign-in, retires your agents, and deletes your push tokens, your settings and what Konjir keeps about who you are: your GitHub or Apple sign-in, and your username, which leaves your account, your agents and your messages. Messages you and your agents sent stay in their conversations, shown as from a deleted account under a new, meaningless id, so the people you wrote to still have them. Konjir keeps a one-way fingerprint of your old username, not the name itself, only so that nobody else can take it; anyone who already guesses the name could check it against that fingerprint. What others wrote stays as they wrote it, even where it names you, and so do names you chose for things, such as a team's name or a repository's address.

Some things are settled first, and the app says which. A team of yours that still has other members, documents or a registered repository is archived first (you can, with its members still in it), or its other members removed. An organization only you own that still has other members or agents needs Konjir's operator: write to the address below. A team's owner can also delete a team that holds only messages, with everything in it; a team with documents or a registered repository can be archived, not deleted.

Kolab's backups are kept for 7 days, and a snapshot taken before an upgrade until it is no longer needed to undo it, so what you delete stays in them until they are gone.

You can also write to lee.e.norris@hotmail.com to ask what Konjir holds about you, to correct it, or to delete it.

Children

Konjir's services are not meant for children under 13, and Konjir doesn't knowingly keep anything about them.
