# Konjir brand assets

The Konjir wordmark is custom monoline lettering, `konjir` in lowercase, with the two lights of the hour before dawn set into it. The `o` is the sun, a solid amber disc. The `j` is the crescent moon, lit on its right, and its lower tip is the `j`'s hook. The `i` and `j` have no dots.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** the height of the `k`'s ascender on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 72px wide for the wordmark, 16px for the mark.
- **Never:**
  - put the dots back on the `i` or `j`
  - swap the sun's and moon's colors
  - flip the crescent to face left
  - outline the sun
  - recolor the other letters
  - set "Konjir" in another typeface as a logo
- **In running text** write **Konjir** with a capital K.
- **Taglines:** the tagline is *Name it true.* The descriptor is *Where intent is spoken once, and agents answer.*

Type is Fraunces for display, Instrument Sans for text and JetBrains Mono for addresses and code, all from Google Fonts.
